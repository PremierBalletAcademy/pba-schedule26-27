Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how information is handled when you use the Premier Ballet Academy 2026/27 Schedule website.

Effective August 28, 2026

1. About This Website

The Premier Ballet Academy 2026/27 Schedule website provides information about classes, teachers, studios, schedules and related academy activities.

The website may also provide an optional feature allowing users to add selected Premier Ballet Academy classes to their personal Google Calendar.

2. Information We May Receive

Most of the schedule can be viewed without providing personal information.

If you choose to use the Google Calendar feature, Google may ask you to sign in and authorize the website to use the permissions required for that feature.

Depending on the permissions requested, this may include basic Google account information and permission to create calendar events in the Google Calendar account you authorize.

3. How Information Is Used

Information associated with Google authorization is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, such as adding selected Premier Ballet Academy classes to Google Calendar.

We do not use Google account information obtained through the calendar feature for advertising or behavioral profiling.

We do not sell personal information obtained through this website.

4. Google Calendar Access

Connecting Google Calendar is completely optional. You can continue to use the schedule without connecting your Google account.

When you authorize Google Calendar access, the authorization is handled through Google's authentication and authorization systems. You can review or revoke permissions granted to an application through your Google Account.

The website is intended to use calendar access only for the calendar functionality requested by the user.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services to provide hosting, authentication, calendar functionality, or other technical features.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

The principal third-party services used for the website may include Google services and GitHub Pages.

6. Information Sharing

Premier Ballet Academy does not sell or rent personal information obtained through the website.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide features that the user chooses to use, such as Google Calendar authorization.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the website and its features.

However, no website, online service, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The schedule website is primarily intended for parents, teachers, academy staff and other users who need access to academy schedule information.

The website is not designed to use children's personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to connect Google Calendar and may use the schedule without granting Google permissions.

  • You may decline Google authorization when prompted.
  • You may revoke previously granted Google permissions through your Google Account.
  • You may contact Premier Ballet Academy regarding questions about privacy and the website.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its features, or its data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Premier Ballet Academy schedule website, please contact Premier Ballet Academy through its official academy contact channels.
